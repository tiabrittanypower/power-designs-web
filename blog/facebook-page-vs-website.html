Facebook and your website

Facebook page vs website: why your business needs both

Facebook is excellent at helping people discover you. A website gives them a reliable place to verify, understand and contact your business.

By Tia Power4 July 20266 min read

Facebook Marketplace, community groups and business pages can be powerful sources of leads. The problem begins when your entire online presence belongs to a platform you do not control.

Facebook is rented space

Platform rules, algorithms and account access can change. Your posts quickly disappear down a feed, and customers may struggle to find essential information again.

Your website is your permanent address

A website organises your services, prices, service areas, contact information and frequently asked questions in one dependable place. You control the presentation and the customer journey.

Social media starts conversations. Your website gives those conversations somewhere credible to land.

Why customers check both

A potential customer may discover you on Facebook and then search your business name. A clear website, matching contact details and professional email address reduce uncertainty and help confirm that the business is genuine.

Use each channel for what it does best

  • Facebook: reach, conversation, community and regular updates.
  • Your website: credibility, structure, search visibility and conversions.
  • WhatsApp: quick personal communication once interest exists.

Start without abandoning what already works

A website does not replace Facebook Marketplace. It strengthens it. Add the website link to every listing, page and profile so interested people can quickly see your services, process and contact options.
