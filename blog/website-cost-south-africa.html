How much does a small-business website cost in South Africa?

The short answer is anywhere from a few hundred rand per month to tens of thousands once-off. The useful answer depends on what is actually included.

By Tia Power4 July 20268 min read

A low price is not automatically a bargain, and a high price is not automatically evidence of quality. South African website quotes vary because providers may be selling completely different things under the same label.

Typical small-business price ranges

A simple one-to-three-page website often starts around R3,000 to R6,000. A professional five-page business website commonly falls between roughly R5,500 and R16,000. Larger, custom or strategy-led websites can cost considerably more.

Subscription offers are also common. These lower the upfront cost but may affect ownership, minimum terms and what happens when you stop paying.

Compare the scope, ownership and ongoing costs—not only the number printed at the bottom.

What should be included?

  • Mobile-friendly design
  • Clear page structure and navigation
  • Contact or enquiry functionality
  • Basic search-engine setup
  • SSL security
  • A defined number of revision rounds
  • Clear ownership and handover terms

Costs beyond the initial build

Your website still needs a domain and hosting. You may also choose maintenance, business email, premium software, SEO, paid advertising or ongoing content support. None of these are inherently suspicious—but they should be disclosed before you commit.

Once-off or monthly?

A once-off project usually costs more upfront but can provide clearer ownership. A monthly plan can make a website more accessible and may include hosting and support. Ask whether there is a minimum term and what you retain if the agreement ends.

How to compare two quotes

  1. Count the pages and included features.
  2. Check whether copywriting and imagery are included.
  3. Confirm the number of revisions.
  4. Ask who owns the domain and website.
  5. Calculate the full first-year cost.
  6. Clarify support after launch.
