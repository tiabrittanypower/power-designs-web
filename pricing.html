How does payment work?

Your payment schedule and due dates are stated clearly in the quotation before work begins. Standard terms are 50% deposit and 50% before launch, unless your quote says otherwise. Full client terms (including refunds and ownership) are in our service agreement.

Can I pay in instalments?

Payment plans can be discussed. Websites purchased on a payment plan must be hosted with Power Designs Web until the agreed payments are complete.

Are there hidden monthly fees?

No. Support plans are optional unless they form part of an agreed payment plan. Third-party costs such as domains, premium software or paid booking services are disclosed separately.

Do these prices include VAT?

Power Designs Web is not currently VAT registered, so VAT is not added to the listed prices.
