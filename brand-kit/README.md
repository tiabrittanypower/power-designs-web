# Power Designs Web brand kit

What the brand looks like, in one folder. Source of truth for colours and fonts is `03_Colours_and_Fonts/website-stylesheet-reference.css` (a copy of the live `styles.css`).

- **Colours:** Deep navy #071A3D, Electric blue #246BFE, Gold #F6B73C (accent), Logo coral #FF5A55 (logo square only). Neutrals: Cloud #F5F7FB, Line #DBE3F0, Muted #5E6B82, Ink #12213D, White.
- **Fonts:** Manrope (headings), DM Sans (body). Both free on Google Fonts. Fallback: Arial.
- **Voice:** plain, warm, direct. Sentence case. South African English. Rand prices with "once-off" and "No VAT".
- **Tagline:** Your business is real. Let's make it look the part.
- **Contact:** WhatsApp +27 50 050 4340 · info@powerdesign.co.za · powerdesign.co.za

Folders follow section 17 of the manual (01 to 11). Folders 04, 05, 08, 09, 10 and 11 are empty until templates, stationery, photos, print jobs, licences and archived versions exist.

This folder is not served on the website: `.htaccess` blocks the `brand-kit` path.
