Privacy policy

How Power Designs Web handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Power Designs Web (“we”, “us”) is a remote studio based in South Africa. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website. Because we are based in South Africa, we follow South Africa’s POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act) principles for all visitors, including those in the US. Project work with clients is also covered by our Client service agreement.

Information we collect

When you contact us or send an inquiry (including through the website form, which is delivered via FormSubmit to our business email), we may receive your name, business name, email address, telephone number, project requirements, package preference and preferred contact method.

If you arrive from a campaign or channel link, we may also capture marketing attribution parameters (for example UTM source, medium and campaign, and where present click identifiers such as gclid or fbclid), the page you landed on, and the external referring website. Last-touch details for the current visit are kept in session storage; first-touch details may remain in local storage until you clear site data. These fields may be attached to your inquiry so we know which channel worked.

Why we use it

We use this information to respond to inquiries, prepare quotations, deliver agreed services, keep appropriate business records, understand which marketing channels bring inquiries, and meet legal obligations. We do not use your information for unrelated marketing without a clear reason to contact you about your request.

How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only where reasonably necessary to operate the website, communicate with you (for example email), process form delivery, or deliver an agreed service. Where optional web analytics is enabled (see below), limited technical data may be processed by the analytics provider. Where required by law, we may also disclose information to authorities.

Some of these providers (for example Google, Meta and our form-delivery service) may store or process information outside South Africa. Information you send us from the US is transferred to and handled in South Africa. We only use providers that apply reasonable data-protection safeguards.

Cookies and analytics

Essential / functional storage. The website may use essential cookies or browser storage required for basic operation. Marketing attribution (UTM parameters and a simple channel label such as “marketplace” or “facebook”) may be stored locally so we can measure which channels lead to inquiries and attach that context to form submissions. First-touch data is kept in browser localStorage as pdw_first_touch; last-touch for the visit in sessionStorage as pdw_last_touch. This works without Google Analytics and is only used to improve our marketing and respond to you — we do not sell it.

Optional Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Meta Pixel. If we enable them by configuring a measurement ID and/or Meta Pixel ID on the live site, Google and/or Meta may process technical usage data (for example pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP, device/browser type, and referral or campaign parameters) on our behalf. Scripts only load when the relevant ID is configured; if empty, no Google Analytics or Meta Pixel tags are loaded. On successful inquiry we may also send a lead/conversion event. Processing is subject to those providers’ terms and privacy notices. Where appropriate for non-essential analytics, we may add a consent mechanism.

You can limit tracking via your browser settings, extensions, or Google’s / Meta’s tools for ad personalization. Clearing site data ends the attribution stored in your browser.

Retention and security

Inquiries that do not become projects are deleted after 12 months. Client project, quotation and payment records are kept for 5 years for tax and legal purposes, then deleted. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Email marketing

Any marketing email we send includes our contact details and a simple way to unsubscribe, and we stop emailing you when you opt out (in line with the US CAN-SPAM Act).

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal record-keeping requirements. You may also ask us to stop contacting you about a closed inquiry.

If you are not happy with how we handled your information, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) through its eServices portal at eservices.inforegulator.org.za or email enquiries@inforegulator.org.za.
