How does payment work?

Your payment schedule and due dates are stated clearly in the quotation before work begins. Standard terms are 50% deposit and 50% before launch, unless your quote says otherwise. US clients pay in US dollars by PayPal invoice, unless your quote says otherwise. Full client terms (including refunds and ownership) are in our service agreement.

Can I pay in installments?

Payment plans can be discussed. Websites purchased on a payment plan must be hosted with Power Designs Web until the agreed payments are complete.

Are there hidden monthly fees?

No. Support plans are optional unless they form part of an agreed payment plan. Third-party costs such as domains, premium software or paid booking services are disclosed separately.

Are taxes included?

Power Designs Web is not currently VAT registered, so no VAT is added to the listed prices. The price in your quote is the price you pay for our work.

Where are you based?

We are a small, fully remote studio based in South Africa (SAST, UTC+2), serving US small businesses by email, video call and messaging. We reply within one business day. We don’t have a US office or phone number.
