Straight answers

Questions you may already be asking.

How long will my website take?

A straightforward small-business website normally takes between two and four weeks after we receive the required content and confirm the project. Larger or specialized websites may take longer.

Will I own my website?

Yes. Your domain should be registered in your name, and ownership arrangements will be explained before work begins. If you use an installment plan, hosting with us is required until the plan is complete.

Can you write the words for me?

Yes. Copywriting assistance is included in Grow, while Lead includes fuller copywriting support. We gather the facts from you and shape them into clear, persuasive website copy.

What happens after launch?

You can manage the website yourself or choose an optional support plan for hosting, backups and a finite number of monthly updates.

Where are you based, and how do we work together?

Power Designs Web is a small studio based in South Africa (SAST, UTC+2) and works with US businesses fully remotely, by email, video call and messaging. We reply within one business day, and calls can be arranged around your hours where possible. We don’t have a US office or phone number.

What is the free 10-minute website health check?

Send us your business name and your website or social page. We look at how you show up online and tell you honestly whether a simple website would help right now. It is free and there is no obligation.

Will I appear first on Google?

No ethical web designer can promise a number-one ranking. We build a strong technical and content foundation, but rankings also depend on competition, location, reviews, content and ongoing marketing.
