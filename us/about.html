Meet the founder

Five-plus years of experience, with one clear focus.

I’m Tia Power, founder of Power Designs Web. I create websites that help businesses present themselves professionally, explain their value clearly and make it easier for customers to get in touch.

Power Designs Web is a one-person studio based in South Africa (SAST, UTC+2) and works fully remotely with US small businesses, by email and video call. The approach is personal and straightforward: understand the business, build what it genuinely needs and keep the process transparent from the first conversation to launch.

  • Direct communication with the person building your site
  • Clear pricing and practical recommendations
  • Solutions shaped around your customers
  • Capability across informational, booking and custom websites
